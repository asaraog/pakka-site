Booking and advance payments, inside WhatsApp.

Pakka is software for small businesses in India that already take bookings over WhatsApp and already ask for a UPI advance. It automates what the owner is doing by hand.

What it does

A customer messages the business on WhatsApp, in Hindi or Hinglish, the way they already do. Pakka reads the message, checks the calendar, and offers the times that are free. The owner sees the request on their own phone and approves or declines with one tap.

Once approved, the customer receives a UPI link for the advance. That payment goes directly to the business's own UPI ID. Pakka never receives, holds, or routes the money.

Who it is for

Sports grounds, turf and court operators, clinics, salons, and other appointment-led businesses with one to three bookable resources — the ones running on phone calls, a paper register, and a WhatsApp thread.

What Pakka is not

  • Not a marketplace. We do not list your business, send you customers, or take a cut of a booking. Your customers are yours.
  • Not a payment service. Advances are paid by the customer straight to the business's own UPI ID. We are not a party to that transaction and hold no customer funds at any point.
  • Not automatic. A booking is confirmed only when the business owner approves it. The software never commits your calendar on your behalf.

Onboarding is done by hand, one business at a time. Send us a message from the contact page and we will set up your calendar, your pricing, and your UPI details with you.
