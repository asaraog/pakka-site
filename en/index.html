Bookings and advances — both on WhatsApp.

Your customer messages. You just say yes.

Pakka automates the bookings you already run by hand on WhatsApp. Nothing is confirmed without your yes.

What it does

A customer messages your WhatsApp number the way they already do, in Hindi or Hinglish. Pakka reads the message, checks your calendar, and offers the free times. The request lands on your phone — ✓ पक्का or ✗ नहीं, one tap.

On approval the customer gets a UPI link for the advance. The payment goes directly to your own UPI ID — Pakka never receives, holds, or routes the money.

Who it is for

Turf and sports grounds, courts, clinics, salons — appointment-led businesses with one to three bookable resources, running today on calls, a paper register, and a WhatsApp thread.

What Pakka is not

  • Not a marketplace. We do not list your business, send you customers, or take a cut of a booking. Your customers are yours.
  • Not a payment service. Advances are paid by the customer straight to the business's own UPI ID. We are not a party to that transaction and hold no customer funds at any point.
  • Not automatic. A booking is confirmed only when the business owner approves it. The software never commits your calendar on your behalf.

Getting started

Onboarding is done by hand, one business at a time. Send us a message from the contact page and we will set up your calendar, your pricing, and your UPI details with you.
